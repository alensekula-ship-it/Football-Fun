Football Fun Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Football Fun is developed and published by AS App Factory. This Privacy Policy explains how Football Fun handles information when you use the application.

1. Information stored on your device

Football Fun may store user preferences locally on your device, including selected language, theme, favorite clubs, national teams, followed competitions, followed matches, notification preferences and other personalization settings.

This information is primarily used to provide the features and personalization you request.

2. Notifications

If you enable match reminders or other notifications, Football Fun uses Android notification and alarm functionality to deliver the notifications you selected. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or in your device settings.

3. Football data and network access

Football Fun connects to online football data services to retrieve fixtures, results, standings, club information and other football-related information. Network requests may expose standard technical information such as your IP address to the service provider as part of normal internet communication.

4. Google Play subscriptions and purchases

Football Fun may offer optional Football Fun Pro subscriptions or other digital purchases through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Football Fun does not directly receive or store your full payment card details. Google may provide the app with purchase status and subscription entitlement information so that purchased features can be activated or restored.

5. Advertising

Football Fun may introduce advertising in the free version in the future. If advertising services are added, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosure will be updated before those services are enabled.

6. Personal accounts

Football Fun currently does not require users to create a Football Fun account in order to use the main application features.

7. Data sharing

Football Fun does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services strictly where required to provide app functionality, such as Google Play for purchases and external football data providers for football information.

8. Data security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the application. Information stored locally is subject to the security protections of your Android device and operating system.

9. Children's privacy

Football Fun is a general football information application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Football Fun develops, including when new services such as advertising, subscriptions or additional online features are introduced. The latest version will always be available at this page.
